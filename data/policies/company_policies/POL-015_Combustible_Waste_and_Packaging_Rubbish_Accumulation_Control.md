# POL-015: Combustible Waste and Packaging Rubbish Accumulation Control
**Compliance Reference:** Clause 15.3: Combustible Material Housekeeping | **Category:** Housekeeping & Fire

1. Daily Housekeeping: Discarded shrink wrap, corrugated cardboard scrap, wooden dunnage, and broken strapping bands must not accumulate in operating aisles.
2. Baler Operations: Cardboard and clean plastic film must be transferred to the pneumatic balers at least twice per 8-hour shift.
3. Fire Risk Mitigation: Accumulation of combustible packaging within 5 meters of electrical panels or charging stations is classified as a severe fire hazard.
4. End-of-Shift Signoff: Shift supervisors must walk all picking aisles and certify 100% floor clearance before handing over facility custody.
