# POL-012: Inbound Trailer Security Seals and Freight Discrepancy Audits
**Compliance Reference:** Clause 12.4: Inbound Freight Seal Verification | **Category:** Logistics & Compliance

1. Seal Inspection: Before cutting any inbound trailer bolt seal, the receiving clerk must photograph the seal and verify the numeric identifier against the bill of lading.
2. Broken or Missing Seals: Any trailer arriving with a broken, missing, or mismatched seal must not be unloaded until an EHS supervisor and security lead conduct an audit.
3. Manifest Comparison: Declared pallet count and hazardous cargo designations must be validated against the physical load before issuing receipt signatures.
4. Retention: Seal audit photos and paperwork manifests are archived in the digital document repository for 12 months.
