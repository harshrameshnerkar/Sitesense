# POL-006: Hazardous Chemical Spill Containment and Emergency Response
**Compliance Reference:** Clause 6.2: Hazardous Fluid Spill Response | **Category:** Environmental & Chemical

1. Immediate Notification: Any liquid spill exceeding 1 liter of chemical, solvent, hydraulic fluid, or fuel requires immediate evacuation of the affected aisle.
2. Spill Kit Deployment: Universal absorbent socks, pads, and non-sparking scoops must be deployed within 5 minutes of identification.
3. PPE for Cleanup: Spill response personnel must wear nitrile chemical-resistant gauntlets, splash goggles, and half-face respirators with organic vapor cartridges.
4. Disposal: Spent absorbent materials must be sealed in yellow hazardous waste recovery drums with completed manifests.
