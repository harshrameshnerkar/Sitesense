# POL-011: Overhead Conveyor Systems and Nip-Point Safety Guarding
**Compliance Reference:** Clause 11.1: Mechanical Conveyor Safety | **Category:** Machinery Guarding

1. Guarding: All chain drives, rollers, and belt pinch points along automated package sorting lines must be fitted with fixed metal mesh safety guards.
2. Emergency Stops: Continuous emergency pull cords (e-stop cables) must run the entire length of overhead conveyor lines within reach of floor personnel.
3. De-jamming Protocol: Under no circumstances may an employee reach into a running conveyor to dislodge cartons. Lockout/Tagout (LOTO) is mandatory before jam clearing.
4. Overhead Nets: Conveyors traversing pedestrian walkways must have side containment netting to catch falling packages.
