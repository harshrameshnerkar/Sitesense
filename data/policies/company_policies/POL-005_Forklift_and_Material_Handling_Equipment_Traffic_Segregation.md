# POL-005: Forklift and Material Handling Equipment Traffic Segregation
**Compliance Reference:** Clause 5.1: MHE Traffic Segregation | **Category:** Equipment Operations

1. Segregation Rules: Forklifts, order pickers, and reach trucks must operate strictly in designated vehicle travel lanes marked with blue safety lines.
2. Pedestrian Buffer: Operators must maintain a minimum 3.0-meter (10 feet) safe operating buffer from any pedestrian walking in shared staging zones.
3. Speed Limits: Maximum speed within enclosed warehouse aisles is 8 km/h (5 mph); loading dock threshold speed is 3 km/h (2 mph).
4. Blind Corner Protocol: Operators must sound their horn and come to a complete rolling stop at all blind T-junctions and corridor entrances.
