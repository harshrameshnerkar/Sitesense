# POL-003: Mandatory Personal Protective Equipment Protocols
**Compliance Reference:** Clause 3.4: Mandatory Personal Protective Equipment | **Category:** Personnel Safety

1. Scope: Applies to all employees, contractors, and visitors entering active warehouse operational zones.
2. Core Requirements: High-visibility fluorescent vests (ANSI Class 2 minimum) and steel-toe composite safety footwear (ASTM F2413 compliant) are mandatory facility-wide.
3. Loading Bay & High-Bay Specifics: Inbound dock areas, high-rack storage aisles, and active forklift corridors require certified industrial safety helmets (hard hats compliant with ANSI Z89.1).
4. Enforcement: Personnel observed without required hard hats in loading docks must be halted immediately and escorted to the safety locker station.
