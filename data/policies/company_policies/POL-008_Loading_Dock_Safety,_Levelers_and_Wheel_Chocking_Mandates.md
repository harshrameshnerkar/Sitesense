# POL-008: Loading Dock Safety, Levelers and Wheel Chocking Mandates
**Compliance Reference:** Clause 8.3: Dock Safety & Chocking | **Category:** Dock Logistics

1. Vehicle Restraint: Semi-trailers backed into loading bays 1 through 8 must have automated dock lock restraints engaged or dual rubber wheel chocks positioned.
2. Dock Leveler Operation: Hydraulic dock levelers must be inspected for lip engagement before any forklift or hand truck traverses the trailer threshold plate.
3. Fall Protection: Dock bay doors that remain open without a trailer docked must have the yellow high-visibility safety barrier chain securely latched across the opening.
4. Driver Staging: Truck drivers must wait in the designated driver safety lounge and surrender trailer keys during all unloading cycles.
