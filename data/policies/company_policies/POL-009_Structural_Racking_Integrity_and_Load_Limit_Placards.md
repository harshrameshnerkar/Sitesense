# POL-009: Structural Racking Integrity and Load Limit Placards
**Compliance Reference:** Clause 9.1: Pallet Racking Integrity | **Category:** Structural Engineering

1. Load Capacities: Every selective pallet racking bay must feature visible yellow load capacity placards specifying maximum allowable weight per beam pair (e.g. 2,500 kg).
2. Damage Criteria: Any rack upright exhibiting deflection greater than 3 mm across a 1-meter span from forklift impact must be tagged with a RED out-of-service placard.
3. Unloading Mandate: Damaged racking bays must be completely depalletized within 2 hours of structural damage discovery.
4. Beam Safety Pins: Every beam connection must have safety locking pins engaged to prevent accidental dislodgement during pallet lifting.
