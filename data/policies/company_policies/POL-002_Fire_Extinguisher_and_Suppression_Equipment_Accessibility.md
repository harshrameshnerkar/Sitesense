# POL-002: Fire Extinguisher and Suppression Equipment Accessibility
**Compliance Reference:** Clause 2.1: Fire Safety Equipment Access | **Category:** Fire & Life Safety

1. Objective & Scope: Guarantee immediate access to portable fire extinguishers and suppression control valves.
2. Physical Clearance: A minimum clearance radius of 1.0 meter (36 inches) must be maintained around every wall-mounted fire extinguisher, hose station, and fire sprinkler riser valve.
3. Floor Demarcation: The floor area below each fire extinguisher must be painted with alternating red and white diagonal safety stripes.
4. Prohibited Acts: Storing inventory, leaning ladders, or stacking pallets in front of extinguishers is a Class-A critical safety violation requiring immediate supervisor intervention.
