# POL-019: Chemical Drum Secondary Containment Sump Standards
**Compliance Reference:** Clause 19.1: Chemical Drum Storage | **Category:** Hazardous Materials

1. Secondary Containment: All 55-gallon (208L) drums containing hazardous chemicals, lubricants, or paints must rest on certified polyethylene secondary spill pallets.
2. Sump Capacity: The secondary containment sump must have a minimum holding capacity equal to 110% of the single largest drum or 25% of total volume stored.
3. Incompatible Materials: Acids and bases, or flammables and oxidizers, must be segregated into distinct containment bays separated by a minimum of 6 meters.
4. Inspection Frequency: Sumps must be visually checked daily for fluid collection. Any accumulated rainwater or chemical must be pumped out and disposed of.
