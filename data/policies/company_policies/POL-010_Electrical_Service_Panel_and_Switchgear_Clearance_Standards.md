# POL-010: Electrical Service Panel and Switchgear Clearance Standards
**Compliance Reference:** Clause 10.2: Electrical Equipment Clearances | **Category:** Electrical Safety

1. OSHA 1910.303 Compliance: All electrical breaker panels, transformers, and motor disconnect switches require a minimum 36-inch (91 cm) deep, unobstructed frontal clearance zone.
2. Floor Striping: Electrical clearance zones must be bounded by yellow safety paint on the warehouse floor with the warning 'ELECTRICAL PANEL - KEEP CLEAR AT ALL TIMES'.
3. Staging Ban: No pallets, rolling carts, waste bins, or cleaning equipment may rest inside this envelope, even momentarily.
4. Qualified Personnel: Only licensed electrician contractors or certified maintenance engineers may open energized switchgear enclosures.
