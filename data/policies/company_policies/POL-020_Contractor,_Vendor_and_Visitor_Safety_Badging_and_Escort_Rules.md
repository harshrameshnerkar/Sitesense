# POL-020: Contractor, Vendor and Visitor Safety Badging and Escort Rules
**Compliance Reference:** Clause 20.3: Visitor & Contractor Badging | **Category:** Site Security & Governance

1. Check-In & Safety Briefing: All non-badged visitors and third-party maintenance contractors must register at the security desk and complete the 5-minute safety orientation.
2. Badging: High-visibility RED contractor visitor badges must be worn conspicuously on outer garments above the waist.
3. Mandatory Escorts: Visitors are strictly prohibited from walking onto active logistics warehouse floors without an authorized SiteSense employee escort.
4. PPE Provision: The receiving team is responsible for providing compliant hard hats, high-vis vests, and safety glasses to visitors before floor entry.
