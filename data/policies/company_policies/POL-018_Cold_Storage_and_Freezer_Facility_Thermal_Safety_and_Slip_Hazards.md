# POL-018: Cold Storage and Freezer Facility Thermal Safety and Slip Hazards
**Compliance Reference:** Clause 18.4: Cold Storage Safety | **Category:** Specialized Environments

1. Thermal Gear: Employees entering deep-freeze areas (-20°C to -25°C) must wear certified insulated sub-zero coveralls, thermal boots, and fleece-lined gloves.
2. Exposure Limits: No worker may remain continuously inside freezer chambers for more than 90 minutes without a 15-minute warming break in the ambient vestibule.
3. Ice & Frost Abatement: Ice buildup on freezer thresholds or walkway ramps must be mechanically scraped and treated with approved calcium chloride pellets daily.
4. Emergency Release: All freezer doors must feature luminous internal mechanical emergency push-to-exit plunger latches that cannot be locked from the outside.
