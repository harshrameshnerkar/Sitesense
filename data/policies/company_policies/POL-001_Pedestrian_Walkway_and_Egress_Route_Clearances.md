# POL-001: Pedestrian Walkway and Egress Route Clearances
**Compliance Reference:** Clause 1.2: Egress Route Clearances | **Category:** Egress & Facility Traffic

1. Objective & Scope: To ensure all pedestrian thoroughfares and emergency exit pathways remain 100% unobstructed.
2. Standards: All designated pedestrian walkways marked with yellow or reflective epoxy floor striping must maintain a minimum clear width of 1.2 meters (48 inches) at all times.
3. Strict Prohibitions: No temporary or permanent staging of wooden pallets, corrugated carton boxes, plastic totes, or material handling equipment is permitted inside walkway boundaries under any circumstances.
4. Immediate Corrective Action: Any item detected inside a designated walkway must be tagged and relocated to an approved staging bay within 15 minutes of discovery.
