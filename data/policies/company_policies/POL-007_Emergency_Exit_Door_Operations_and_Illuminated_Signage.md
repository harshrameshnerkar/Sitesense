# POL-007: Emergency Exit Door Operations and Illuminated Signage
**Compliance Reference:** Clause 7.1: Emergency Exit Integrity | **Category:** Life Safety & Egress

1. Exit Door Standards: All designated emergency fire exits must feature panic hardware (crash bars) that open outward with less than 15 pounds of pushing force.
2. Zero Obstruction: A clear, unencumbered space of 2.0 meters on both sides of every exit door is legally mandated. No wedging or propping doors open.
3. Illumination: Overhead EXIT signs must remain continuously illuminated with 90-minute battery backup power in event of main electrical failure.
4. Auditing: Daily visual verification of exit door swing paths is conducted by the morning shift supervisor.
