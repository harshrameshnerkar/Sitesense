# POL-013: Industrial Battery Charging Station Ventilation and Safety
**Compliance Reference:** Clause 13.1: Battery Charging Safety | **Category:** Battery Operations

1. Ventilation: Lead-acid and lithium forklift battery charging zones must feature continuous active exhaust fans to prevent hydrogen gas accumulation (>1% lower explosive limit).
2. Eyewash Proximity: An OSHA-approved emergency eyewash station capable of 15-minute continuous flushing must be located within 25 feet (7.6 meters) of all charging bays.
3. PPE Requirements: Personnel handling battery electrolyte must wear a rubber apron, face shield, and chemical-resistant neoprene gauntlets.
4. No Open Flames: Smoking, welding, and non-intrinsically safe electrical devices are strictly prohibited within a 10-meter radius of the charging room.
