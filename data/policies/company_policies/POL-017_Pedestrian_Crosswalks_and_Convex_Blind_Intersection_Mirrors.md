# POL-017: Pedestrian Crosswalks and Convex Blind Intersection Mirrors
**Compliance Reference:** Clause 17.2: Crosswalk Right-of-Way | **Category:** Traffic Management

1. Pedestrian Right of Way: Pedestrians utilizing painted yellow safety crosswalks have absolute right of way over all forklifts, tuggers, and pallet jacks.
2. Convex Mirrors: Industrial 36-inch convex mirrors must be installed at all four-way warehouse intersections and maintained free of dust and visual obstruction.
3. Stop & Sound: Forklift operators approaching marked crosswalks must decelerate to 3 km/h, tap horn twice, and visually confirm the mirror is clear before proceeding.
4. Floor Stenciling: Crosswalk approaches must feature bold white lettering: 'LOOK BOTH WAYS - FORKLIFT TRAFFIC'.
