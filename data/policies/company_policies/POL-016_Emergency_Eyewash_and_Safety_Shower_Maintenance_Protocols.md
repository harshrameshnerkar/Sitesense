# POL-016: Emergency Eyewash and Safety Shower Maintenance Protocols
**Compliance Reference:** Clause 16.1: Eyewash & Safety Showers | **Category:** Occupational Health

1. Travel Time: Emergency eyewash stations must be accessible within 10 seconds of uninhibited walking from any chemical or battery handling workstation.
2. Weekly Inspection: Stations must be activated and flushed weekly for 3 minutes to verify water pressure, clear bacterial growth, and check temperature (16°C to 38°C).
3. Unobstructed Path: The pathway to eyewash fixtures must remain completely flat, unencumbered by hoses, cords, or freight.
4. Inspection Tagging: A physical laminated inspection tag must be signed and dated by the facility safety technician upon weekly verification.
