# POL-004: Material Stacking Standards and Pallet Height Limits
**Compliance Reference:** Clause 4.1: Material Stacking Standards | **Category:** Material Handling

1. Scope: Dictates allowable vertical height and stability criteria for floor-staged unit loads.
2. Open Floor Stacking Limit: Palletized goods staged directly on the warehouse floor must not exceed 3 tiers (maximum 2.4 meters / 8 feet in total height).
3. Stability Controls: Any pallet stack exceeding tier 1 must be securely shrink-wrapped with corner boards where required. Leaning stacks exceeding 5 degrees vertical tilt are strictly forbidden.
4. Heavy Goods: Pallets containing liquids, chemical drums, or loose heavy machinery parts must remain single-tier unless racked in rated steel uprights.
