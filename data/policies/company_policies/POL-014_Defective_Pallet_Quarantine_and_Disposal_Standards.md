# POL-014: Defective Pallet Quarantine and Disposal Standards
**Compliance Reference:** Clause 14.2: Defective Pallet Quarantine | **Category:** Asset Management

1. Inspection Criteria: Pallets exhibiting cracked deck boards, broken stringers, protruding nails, or severe oil contamination must be taken out of service immediately.
2. Designated Quarantine: Defective units must be moved to the designated 'Pallet Quarantine Corral' located near Bay 12 and tagged with a fluorescent orange notice.
3. Stacking for Disposal: Quarantine pallets may be stacked up to a maximum of 15 units high in a stable interlocking pattern awaiting timber recycler pickup.
4. Reuse Prohibition: Re-introducing quarantined pallets into high-bay automated racking is grounds for disciplinary review.
