# Freight Receiving, Loading Dock Operations & Yard Safety Handbook

## Section 1: Inbound Yard Traffic Patterns
External tractor-trailers must follow the clockwise yard loop at speeds not exceeding 15 km/h. Backing into dock bays must be guided by dock spotters when visibility is limited.

## Section 2: Dock Door Drop Hazards
Unprotected edges of loading docks exceeding 48 inches in height represent significant fall hazards. Warning gates or safety nets must be deployed across any bay opening whenever a trailer is not positioned.

## Section 3: Material Lift Gate Safety
Hydraulic lift gates on delivery box trucks must be fully lowered and flat on concrete before loading personnel or hand trucks roll onto the platform.
