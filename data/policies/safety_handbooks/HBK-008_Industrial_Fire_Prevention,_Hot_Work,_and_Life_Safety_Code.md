# HBK-008: Industrial Fire Prevention, Hot Work, and Life Safety Code
**Standards Code:** NFPA 1 & NFPA 13 Compliance Manual

# Industrial Fire Prevention, Hot Work & Life Safety Code

## Chapter 1: Sprinkler Head Clearance (NFPA 13)
A minimum vertical clearance of 18 inches (45 cm) must be maintained below all sprinkler deflectors throughout standard pallet storage. In high-density ESFR storage areas, clearance must be 36 inches.

## Chapter 2: Hot Work Permitting
Any welding, torch cutting, or grinding requires an issued Hot Work Permit signed by the safety supervisor. A dedicated fire watch personnel with a 10-lb ABC extinguisher must monitor the site for 60 minutes post-work.

## Chapter 3: Flammable Liquid Storage Cabinets
Flammable liquids in quantities greater than 5 gallons must be stored in double-walled self-closing yellow FM-approved safety cabinets with three-point door latches.
