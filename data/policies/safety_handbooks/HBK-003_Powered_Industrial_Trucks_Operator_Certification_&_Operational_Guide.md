# HBK-003: Powered Industrial Trucks Operator Certification & Operational Guide
**Standards Code:** OSHA 1910.178 & ANSI B56.1

# Powered Industrial Trucks Operator Certification & Operational Guide

## Section 1: Pre-Shift Inspection Mandate
Operators must perform and log a documented pre-shift physical inspection covering hydraulic systems, brakes, steering, horn, backup alarm, and fork condition. Any vehicle with safety deficiencies must be immediately locked out.

## Section 2: Traveling and Speed Management
Loads must be tilted backward and carried as low to the ground as safely possible (4 to 6 inches off floor). When ascending or descending grades in excess of 10 percent, loaded trucks shall be driven with the load upgrade.

## Section 3: Dockboard and Trailer Safety
Prior to driving onto any truck or trailer, operators must visually confirm that the trailer wheels are securely chocked or the automated vehicle restraint system is locked.
