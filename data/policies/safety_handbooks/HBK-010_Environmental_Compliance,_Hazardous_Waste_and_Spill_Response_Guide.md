# Environmental Compliance, Hazardous Waste & Spill Response Guide

## Section 1: EPA Resource Conservation and Recovery (RCRA)
Hazardous wastes generated from cleaning solvents, expired paint, and battery acids must be labeled 'HAZARDOUS WASTE' with accumulation start dates clearly inscribed on exterior drum faces.

## Section 2: Stormwater Discharge Protection
Outdoor dock drains and retention pond spillways must be protected with storm drain filter inserts. Chemical washing of trucks or pallets in exterior yards is strictly banned.

## Section 3: Waste Manifest Documentation
All hazardous waste hauling requires signed EPA Uniform Hazardous Waste Manifests. Copies must be countersigned by certified waste transporters and retained for 3 years.
