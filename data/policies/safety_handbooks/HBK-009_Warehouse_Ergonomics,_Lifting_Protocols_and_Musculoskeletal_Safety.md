# Warehouse Ergonomics, Lifting Protocols & Musculoskeletal Safety

## Rule 1: The 23 kg (50 lb) Maximum Single-Person Lift
No single warehouse associate is permitted to lift any object exceeding 23 kg (50 lbs) unassisted. Loads exceeding this limit require mandatory dual-person team lifting or mechanical hoist equipment.

## Rule 2: Biomechanical Lifting Technique
Lift with the legs and core, keeping the load within the 'power zone' (mid-thigh to mid-chest). Avoid twisting the torso while carrying unit loads; reposition feet to turn.

## Rule 3: High-Frequency Picking Rotation
Order pickers performing continuous manual carton selection must rotate workstations every 2 hours to avoid repetitive strain on shoulder and lumbar muscle groups.
