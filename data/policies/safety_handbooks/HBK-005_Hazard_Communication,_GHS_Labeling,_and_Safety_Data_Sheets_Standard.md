# HBK-005: Hazard Communication, GHS Labeling, and Safety Data Sheets Standard
**Standards Code:** 29 CFR 1910.1200 HazCom

# Hazard Communication & Chemical Safety Guide

## 1. Globally Harmonized System (GHS) Labeling
All chemical containers, including secondary squeeze bottles and sprayers, must display standardized GHS pictograms, signal words (DANGER or WARNING), hazard statements, and manufacturer contact details.

## 2. Safety Data Sheet (SDS) Access
Digital and physical SDS binders must be accessible 24/7 without password restriction to all warehouse personnel in the EHS kiosk near the shipping office.

## 3. Chemical Spill Kit Placement
Spill response stations equipped with chemical-neutralizing absorbents and PPE must be positioned every 30 meters along chemical staging corridors.
