# HBK-006: Heavy-Duty Pallet Racking Inspection, Maintenance and Load Limits Manual
**Standards Code:** RMI ANSI MH16.1 Design Code

# Heavy-Duty Pallet Racking Inspection and Maintenance Code

## Article 1: Structural Inspection Standards
Quarterly engineering audits evaluate upright column verticality, diagonal brace damage, and baseplate anchor bolt torque (minimum 65 ft-lbs). Any sheared or loosened anchor requires immediate structural repair.

## Article 2: Beam Deflection Limits
Maximum vertical deflection of horizontal beam pairs under full load must not exceed L/180 of the beam span. Beams exceeding this threshold indicate severe overload and must be depalletized.

## Article 3: Pallet Overhang Guidelines
Standard pallets must have a minimum front-to-back beam overlap of 50 mm (2 inches) on both front and rear beams. Side overhang exceeding 75 mm is strictly prohibited.
