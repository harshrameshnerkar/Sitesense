# OSHA General Industry Warehouse Safety Compliance Handbook
Standard: 29 CFR 1910 Subpart D (Walking-Working Surfaces) & Subpart N (Materials Handling)

## Chapter 1: Walking-Working Surfaces (1910.22)
All places of employment, passageways, storerooms, and service rooms shall be kept clean, orderly, and in a sanitary condition. Floor surfaces must be kept free from hazards such as sharp objects, loose boards, spills, and protruding obstacles. Permanent aisles and passageways must be clearly demarcated with painted striping.

## Chapter 2: Means of Egress (1910.36)
Exit routes must remain unobstructed at all times. Exit paths must not lead through rooms or areas that can be locked or through spaces that contain hazardous storage. Maintenance of fire resistance ratings and visibility of exit signs is mandatory.

## Chapter 3: Materials Handling and Storage (1910.176)
Storage of material shall not create a hazard. Bags, containers, bundles, etc., stored in tiers shall be stacked, blocked, interlocked, and limited in height so that they are stable and secure against sliding or collapse. Aisles and passageways shall be kept clear with no obstructions across doorways.
