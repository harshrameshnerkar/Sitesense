# HBK-002: ISO 45001 Occupational Health and Safety Management System Manual
**Standards Code:** ISO 45001:2018 Management Code

# ISO 45001 Occupational Health & Safety Management Manual
Clause 8: Operational Planning and Control

## 8.1.2 Eliminating Hazards and Reducing OH&S Risks
The organization must establish, implement, and maintain a process for the elimination of hazards and reduction of OH&S risks using the hierarchy of controls: Elimination, Substitution, Engineering Controls, Administrative Controls, and Personal Protective Equipment.

## 8.2 Emergency Preparedness and Response
The organization must maintain structured readiness for potential emergency scenarios, including structural collapse, chemical spills, and fire incidents. Periodic evacuation drills and audit checklists ensure continuous operational compliance.
