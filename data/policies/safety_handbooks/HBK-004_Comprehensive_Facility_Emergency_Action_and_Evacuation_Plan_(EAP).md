# Comprehensive Facility Emergency Action and Evacuation Plan (EAP)

## Part I: Emergency Reporting and Alarm Activation
In the event of active fire, structural compromise, or toxic atmospheric release, the discovering employee must pull the nearest manual fire alarm station and dial emergency extension 5555.

## Part II: Evacuation Protocols
All personnel must immediately drop non-essential tasks and exit via the nearest illuminated emergency egress door. Do not use elevators or material lifts. Floor wardens conduct sweep audits of breakrooms and restrooms.

## Part III: Muster Point Assembly
All building occupants assemble at designated Muster Station Alpha (North Gate Parking) or Muster Station Bravo (South Lawn). Roll-call must be finalized within 8 minutes of alarm initiation.
